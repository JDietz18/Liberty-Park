Option 2

Blue ground, deep shade.

Option 2 puts shade first: navy canopies over the swings and the decks, wheat-colored posts, and blue surfacing across the whole circle.

One circle, three play areas

Paths cross at the center tree.

  1. Towers and dome

    The twin-tower structure shares the widest area with the dome climber, a spinner and balance pieces.

  2. Shaded swings

    One long canopy covers the full run of swings. A smaller canopy stands beside it.

  3. Shaded deck

    The lower, ramped structure sits under two navy canopies.
